## 🔑 Key Contents to Include (Line by Line)
To ensure your legal safety, your policy must include these specific elements:
## 1. Strict Age Restriction Statement

* Absolute Minimum Age: State clearly that the website is strictly for individuals aged 18 and older (or the legal drinking age in their jurisdiction).
* Zero Tolerance: Explicitly state that minors (under 18) are completely prohibited from using, viewing, or interacting with the platform.

## 2. Age Verification Disclaimer

* Mandatory Age Gate: State that users must pass an age verification prompt before entering.
* User Responsibility: Clarify that providing a false birth date or circumventing the age gate is a direct violation of your Terms of Service.

## 3. Parental Responsibility & Account Security

* Parental Control Advice: Include a clause advising parents to use filtering software (like Net Nanny or CyberSitter) to block their children from accessing your URL.
* Shared Device Liability: State that if adults play this game on a single phone in a party setting, it is the adult owner's absolute responsibility to ensure no minors are present or viewing the screen.

## 4. Content Disclaimer & Context

* Adult Themes: Explicitly mention that the platform contains references to alcohol consumption, suggestive themes, and mature challenges.
* No Appeal to Minors: State that the games are designed purely for adult social gatherings and are not marketed, designed, or intended to attract children.

## 5. Data Deletion Protocol (COPPA/GDPR Safe Harbor)

* Accidental Collection: State that you do not knowingly collect any data from individuals under 18.
* Reporting and Removal: Provide a dedicated email address (e.g., safety@turnonyou.today) where parents can report if a minor has accessed the site, promising immediate deletion of that user's data and account block.
